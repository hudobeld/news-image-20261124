A one-image summary of the world's news of **Tuesday 24 November 2026**,
drawn programmatically as a "red night edition": ink black, vermilion and
warm paper. A full-bleed AI-generated globe at night, duotone-treated in
black and vermilion, carries the masthead and an oversized vermilion "24"
bleeding off the left edge; below it a solid vermilion date band, one big
hero story, a 60/40 pair of dark cards, and three inverted paper cards.

## The six stories

1. **The Hague — International Law.** The International Court of Justice
   rules that Myanmar's campaign against the Rohingya people was genocide.
2. **War in Ukraine.** Russia threatens a preventive nuclear strike, as a
   new count puts Russian soldiers killed in the war above 700,000.
3. **Space.** NATO vows to defend against threats in space after reports
   that Russia is preparing a nuclear-armed satellite.
4. **Technology.** Jensen Huang is reinstated as Nvidia chief executive
   after President Trump intervenes.
5. **Health.** The United States loses its measles elimination status,
   held for more than 25 years.
6. **Law / Tech.** US states, led by Oregon and Virginia, sue Elon Musk's
   businesses over sexualised images generated by the Grok chatbot.

All summaries are written in our own words. The banner is AI-generated
in this session and duotone-treated; the pictograms (scales of justice,
warning triangle, satellite, chip, virus, speech bubble) are drawn
programmatically — no photos, no logos, no copied headlines.

## How it runs

`main.ts` copies the poster (`entry.png`, 1024×1024, under 1 MiB) to
`/output/entry.png` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` when set). Node 22,
standard library only, no network.
